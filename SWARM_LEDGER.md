Build a public-source, local-first spatial instrument where people learn the real observer-centred Aura architecture by using it, while genuine capture, measurement and model evidence remains traceable.

## Non-negotiable meanings

- The seven available horn tori share an observer or rig-centred zero point, but scene inspection starts with one active 12 × 24 Green torus.
- Every torus can be shown or hidden independently; visibility never changes its 12 × 24 grammar.
- Each torus has an independent relative outer-envelope control. This scales the canonical horn uniformly; it is not a measured scene distance or an implemented interpersonal boundary.
- The 12 × 24 matrix is a stable address grammar, not a permanent resolution ceiling.
- Exterior `O` and interior `I` faces retain different public/private meanings; labels do not claim a working permissions system.
- Facet-face addresses and directional ray registers are separate ledgers.
- Vision models contribute evidence; they do not replace Aura.
- Original `.360` sources outrank derivatives.
- Unknown or absent evidence remains visibly unknown or absent.
- Start scene perception with one active 12 × 24 torus. The other six are available layers, not simultaneous default interpretations of one scene.
- Canonical horn geometry is the angular address and sampling instrument. Source-scoped range points are evidence along its rays and never deform or replace that geometry.
- The physical horn is drawn only in **Outside horn**. **Inside photo + rays** is the observer-centred 2:1 photosphere plus a display-only continuous angular guide; it opens the coincident horn into two approach-limit poles while leaving all exact source ray registers unchanged.
- A centre search ray continues past the horn to an adaptive display boundary. Away from the horn row, four corner rays show its facet footprint; exact horn-row corner registers remain coincident and are shown separately from the continuous angular patch. The photo sphere is directional imagery and is not called a surface or hit.
- Inside view includes a compact schematic ray profile: zero → horn-address crossing → open search direction → optional range point. The range point remains explicitly labelled “not a hit”, and hiding Search rays hides this profile.
- Scene work follows the honest order: range estimation, surface grouping, segmentation, object detection, then provisional mesh. A later stage cannot be claimed when its input or adapter is absent.
- Evidence from a stitched still belongs to that source record and must not flow to another capture.
- Image collections, MP4 fixtures and native `.360` fixtures are never associated from filenames, timing, nearby storage or visual similarity.
- Capture-to-capture position and yaw remain unregistered; there is no merged site map.
- Runtime and private media stay local: no ChatGPT hosting, media upload or cloud analysis. Public code and approved documentation may be deployed from GitHub.

## Important decisions

- The executable source geometry convention wins over the prose-camera mismatch.
- A horn-row facet shows a continuous parameter patch and exact coincident corner-ray convention separately.
- The at-zero guide uses unwrapped 12 × 24 parameter rectangles, including a final row ending at `v=1` rather than falsely wrapping its display boundary through `v=0`.
- Scan imports remain separately framed and unregistered by default.
- Luke explicitly selected the Aura of Intelligence Public Source Licence for the public repository; it is public source, not open source.
- Local development remains pure Vite on `127.0.0.1`; the public static build is deployed only through GitHub Pages, never ChatGPT hosting.
- Mobile journey navigation scrolls to the working panel and never traps page scrolling inside 3D zoom.
- Display rotation is view state, not a geometry transform, and is disabled by default.
- The image collection, stitched MP4 and native `.360` source are independent fixtures, not a derivative chain.
- Unregistered capture position and yaw prohibit a merged site map or cross-capture placement claim.
- The canonical torus and observed range points are separate records: the torus provides stable angular addresses while evidence supplies distance along selected rays.
- The persisted scene pipeline is ordered as range estimation → surface grouping → segmentation → object detection → provisional mesh. Range is `manual-only`: user-entered evidence plus optional, explicitly labelled neighbour derivations, with no automatic/model adapter. Every later stage is `not-connected` until a real adapter and traceable output exist.

## Unresolved tensions

- A useful dense desktop instrument versus a calm first-time learning flow.
- Mapping physical proximity and interpersonal resonance without collapsing them into one universal scale.
- Relative depth exploration versus the risk that people read it as metric truth.
- Alternative matrices need to expose relationships without becoming coloured duplicates.

## Known gaps

- Native EAC reconstruction and GPMF key decoding are not connected.
- Native `.360` pixels, telemetry keys and depth are not decoded.
- MP4 playback, frame selection and frame-scoped evidence are not connected.
- Automatic single-image range/depth, surface grouping, segmentation, object detection, provisional mesh and normals are not connected. The two-panorama correspondence/pose adapter works as a separate local command but is not connected to browser state.
- No native/working-view relationship is recorded for the independent fixtures; projection transforms remain unverified.
- No simultaneous phone front/rear capture.
- No automatic/model-generated range hypotheses, and no surface groups, segments, detections or reconstructed mesh.
- No GLB output or automatic upstream CLI round-trip.
- Observation reload validation is structural but shallow.
- Metric interpersonal-resonance bands and named boundaries from the supplied prototype screenshot are not implemented. Relative per-torus outer-envelope controls do work, but are not substitutes for those boundaries.

## Do-not-break list

- Source coordinate convention, counts, seam and horn identities.
- Honest status, provenance and uncertainty language.
- Local runtime/private-media boundary and public-source licence.
- Exact scan Z-up to Aura Y-up transform without invented yaw/origin.
- Genuine image pixels as the only input to visible edge evidence.
